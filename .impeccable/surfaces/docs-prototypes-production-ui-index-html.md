# Production UI prototype

Mode: Operate. Scope: design-only, clickable phone-primary and desktop-capable prototype for Today, Plan, Shop, Recipes/detail and Household. Use clearly labeled sample data. Weekly planning and shopping lead. Preserve PRODUCT.md and the established safety, history, explicit-context and confirmation rules. Production app integration is outside this artifact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Community noticeboard clarity makes a household week readable and actionable; dated rows and shopping readiness replace a gallery of equal-weight meal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Public Sans, cobalt shell, pale periwinkle work areas, yellow selected actions and ink text. Flat grouped rows, visible labels and consistent state placement establish the identity.

STORY: Preview seven dinners, understand capacity and leftovers, confirm the week, then shop. Recovery previews consequences before saving; full recipes and household settings remain one destination away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone shows the week heading, draft/save status, dated dinner rows, a persistent confirm-or-shop action and five familiar task destinations. Desktop adds a cobalt navigation rail and a shopping-readiness column. The signature Weekly readiness strip links cooking to leftovers and highlights incomplete groceries. Updates settle with one short state-color transition; reduced motion snaps.

FORM: Grounded candidate 1, user-selected Community noticeboard / Neighborhood Notice; direction seed e7bf206d, kind pick. Code-first confirmed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
